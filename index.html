Executive Summary

CAS correction: the request quoted 11297537-37-1 (extra leading "1"). The valid registry number for 2-chloro-4-(1H-pyrazol-3-yl)benzonitrile is 1297537-37-1 [1]. Use the corrected CAS on all RFQs, POs and Bills of Entry.

This compound is the biaryl "eastern half" of darolutamide (Bayer/Orion's Nubeqa): it carries the 3-chloro-4-cyanophenyl ring and the pyrazole that is later N-alkylated with the (S)-2-aminopropyl arm. Orion's originator process makes it by Suzuki coupling of 4-bromo-2-chlorobenzonitrile with a THP-protected pyrazole-5-boronic ester, then acid deprotection [2]. Demand is therefore tied one-to-one to a fast-growing blockbuster: Nubeqa sales rose 57% to €2.4 bn in 2025 [31] and another 63.9% (Fx-adj.) in Q2-2026 [33].

But volumes are small (single-digit to low-double-digit MT/yr), originator supply is locked into contracted chains, and generic demand stays mostly developmental until patents fall (China core patents Oct-2030; US litigation running to 2038+) [35]. The near-term play is asset-light: import, trade and bundle it with the other darolutamide KSMs already profiled (Boc-L-alaninol, 5-acetyl-1H-pyrazole-3-carboxylic acid, 3-chloro-4-cyanoacetophenone) [44].

The molecule is a small, flat biaryl: a benzonitrile carrying an ortho-chlorine, linked at C-4 to an NH-pyrazole. In darolutamide the pyrazole N-1 is alkylated with an (S)-2-aminopropyl chain and the amine is acylated with 5-(1-hydroxyethyl)-1H-pyrazole-3-carboxylic acid, so this fragment supplies the AR-binding "3-chloro-4-cyanophenyl-pyrazole" headgroup [3]. Its commercial importance is entirely derived from Nubeqa; no other approved drug using it was found.

Strategic Business Frameworks

9A — SWOT (our company entering this product)

Strengths

  • Already profiled the full darolutamide KSM set → one-stop bundle
  • Simple 2-step chemistry, well documented in patents and journals
  • India-based: low-cost CRO/pilot capability, proximity to ANDA filers

Weaknesses

  • No trade data or customer relationships for this CAS yet
  • Boronate starting material imported; Pd chemistry needs scavenging know-how
  • Thin public specs (no mp/SDS) → qualification effort

Opportunities

  • Nubeqa +57–64% growth → originator dual-sourcing
  • Generic wave from Oct-2030 (China) and later US/EU
  • Ref-standard and impurity-synthesis CRO work now
  • Offer Pd-free / non-infringing route to Western generics

Threats

  • Chinese price compression (catalogue already ≈$6/g at 50 g)
  • Patent litigation delaying generics (US to 2038+)
  • Section 232 tariffs; Hormuz freight shocks
  • Customers may integrate backwards (MSN-style captive routes)

Porter's Five Forces

Competitive Rivalry — Medium

Several Chinese sellers, few qualified GMP-like sources; rivalry will rise post-2030.

Threat of New Entrants — Medium

Low technical barrier; customer qualification and FTO are the barriers.

Supplier Power — High

THP-pyrazole-5-boronate and Pd are concentrated inputs; China controls boronate supply.

Buyer Power — High

Few buyers (Orion + a dozen generic API makers), each buying small lots with long qualification.

Threat of Substitutes — Low–Medium

No substitute in darolutamide, but the acetyl route lets API makers build the pyrazole in-house from 3-chloro-4-cyanoacetophenone.

Overall Attractiveness

Moderate. Good for asset-light trading/CRO; weak for standalone capex until generic volumes appear.

Feasibility Summary & Management Recommendation

VERDICT: CONDITIONAL

Enter now through import & domestic trading, sourcing & marketing and CRO services, positioned as part of a complete darolutamide KSM bundle. The product has one solid, fast-growing end use (Nubeqa, €2.4 bn in 2025, still growing ~60%), simple chemistry and no licensing barriers in India. However, merchant volume is only ≈0.5–3 MT/yr today, originator supply is contracted, generic launches are gated by patents (China Oct-2030; US litigation to 2038+), and Chinese catalogue prices are already low. Do not invest in dedicated capacity until a ≥3–5 MT/yr anchor offtake is signed.
